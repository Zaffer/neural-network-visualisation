binary neural network

A neural network with 2 inputs, 2 hidden neurons and 1 output, learning XOR: the output should be 1 when exactly one input is 1.

Each sample goes through one layer at a time: the inputs, then the hidden layer, then the output. After the output, backpropagation nudges every weight and bias to lower the error on that sample. Four samples make an epoch.
